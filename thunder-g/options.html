Expandable message cards, attachments and reply actions in one view. Off by default.

For conversations with 6 or more messages, keep the first and last two visible. Click the counter to show the messages in between. Applies to Thunder-G’s reader when opening a conversation.

How conversations work

Messages are grouped using Thunderbird’s local index, with a folder-thread fallback when indexing is unavailable. Message bodies, attachments, Reply, Forward and Print use Thunderbird’s native viewer and commands.

Changing readers returns to the list. The conversation toolbar acts on messages selected in the list, even when the reader finds additional messages in other folders.

    Import labels from Gmail

    Experimental

    Optional, one-time import of label names and nesting. No emails are downloaded or assigned labels.

    Nothing connects to Google until you choose Connect. Google sign-in opens in your browser and supports 2FA. Google asks to see and edit labels; this importer only reads them. Account identity and label names are fetched directly from Google, with no intermediary server.

    Optional Google connection setup

    A Google Cloud Desktop OAuth client with Gmail API enabled is required. Thunder-G does not yet ship a shared Google client. This feature is optional; other settings work without it.

    Create a Desktop app client, configure its consent screen and (in Testing mode) add your account as a test user. Request only OpenID email and Gmail labels. See Google’s Desktop OAuth setup. Workspace accounts may require administrator approval.

    Credentials and Google access tokens are not saved to extension storage. The downloaded configuration is used only for this connection. Import credentials are separate from your Thunderbird mail accounts.

    Not connected

    Required parent tags are included automatically. Parent and child assignments remain independent. Colors and email assignments are not imported.

    Writing

    Your native composer, with a place in the current window.

    Start in a floating panel. Resize it, expand it beside the sidebar, or open a separate window. Off by default.

    Reply and Reply all start below the conversation. Detach when you need more room. Disable Thunderbird Conversations first. Off by default.

    Use your account’s Drafts folder. Choose Auto to save after a 1.5-second editing pause, or a fixed interval. Empty replies do not create drafts. Enabled by default.

    The editor shows “Saving…”, then “✓ Saved” after confirmation. New edits show “Unsaved changes”; errors show “Save failed”.

    Editor and draft behavior

    Signatures, account identity, recipients, formatting, attachments and draft saving use Thunderbird’s native composer. Forward keeps its usual window. The floating panel resizes from its top or side edge and marked corner; the corner also supports arrow keys. Your chosen size is remembered across messages and restarts, adapting to the available space beside the sidebar.

    An open message is kept when you navigate or start another one. Closing follows native Save, Discard and Cancel prompts. Separate window saves a native draft first. Turning off an in-window option affects new messages and keeps existing work open.

    Autosave changes apply to open and new composers. Disabling Thunder-G restores prior native autosave settings unless they were changed independently.

    Sender logos

    Optional domain logos alongside your messages.

    Looks up the sender’s BIMI DNS record and downloads its advertised HTTPS SVG logo. Off by default.

    This downloads remote images. The logo host can see your IP address. Only the advertised logo is downloaded; no logo lookup service, favicon or certificate download is used.

    Checks, caching and fallback

    Requires a successful aligned DKIM check through the optional integration. A DNS-published logo is not a certified identity badge. Contact photos take priority; missing or invalid records keep the usual avatar.

    Logos and missing results are cached for one hour during this session. Turning this off stops new downloads and restores the usual avatars immediately.
